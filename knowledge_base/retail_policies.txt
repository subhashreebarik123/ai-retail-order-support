RETAIL SHIPPING AND RETURNS POLICY

1. Shipping Policy
Orders are processed after order confirmation. Processing times may vary depending on product availability.
Estimated delivery dates are provided with the order details. Delivery dates are estimates and may change due to logistics delays.
Customers can check their order status using their order ID.

2. Returns Policy
Customers should contact customer support to request a return.
Return eligibility depends on the product category, product condition, and applicable return window.
Products must meet the applicable return conditions before a return can be approved.

3. Refund Policy
Refunds are initiated after the returned product is received and inspected, where inspection is required.
The time taken for a refund to appear depends on the payment method and payment provider.
Customers should contact customer support if a refund is delayed.

4. Order Cancellation Policy
Customers should contact customer support as soon as possible to request cancellation.
Cancellation may not be possible once an order has been shipped.
